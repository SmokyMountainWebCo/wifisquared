Questions

Frequently asked

Is this still the same Wifi Squared?
Yes — same owners, same team, same network since 2013. We've refreshed our look, but your service, billing, and support all continue exactly as before.
How does fixed-wireless internet work?
A small antenna at your home connects to one of our local towers — no cable or phone line needed. That's how we reach places the wired providers never built out to.
Are there contracts or data caps?
No and no. Every plan is month-to-month with unlimited data. You can cancel any time without a fee.
How do I know if service is available at my address?
Call us at (865) 366-4006 or send the contact form and we'll check line-of-sight to our towers from your location — usually within a day.
How do I pay my bill or check my account?
Existing customers can log in through the Customer Login link at the top of the page to view and pay their bill.
