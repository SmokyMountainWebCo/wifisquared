Privacy Policy

Wifi Squared, LLC · (865) 366-4006

Who We Are

Wifi Squared, LLC is an internet service provider serving homes and businesses in East Tennessee. Our website address is https://wifisquared.com. This policy explains what we collect through that website and what we do with it.

What personal data we collect and why we collect it

Availability and contact form

When you check availability or contact us through this website, we collect what you enter: your name, phone number, email address, service address, property type, the plan you are interested in, and anything you tell us about your internet needs. We use it to check coverage at your address, contact you about the options available there, and answer your question.

Form submissions are delivered to us through Formspree, a third-party form processing service, which handles the message on its way to our inbox. Information collected through our forms is kept private and is not shared with anyone outside our company beyond what is needed to deliver and respond to your request.

Text Messages

We do not share, sell, or disclose your personal information or mobile opt-in data to third parties without your explicit consent, except where required by law. Your information is kept confidential and used solely for the purposes you have agreed to.

Consent and Opt-In:

By providing your phone number and opting in to receive text messages, you consent to the collection and use of your personal information as described in this policy. Wifi Squared ensures that your consent is obtained explicitly and that you are informed about the types of messages you will receive. Our voice and text number is (865) 366-4006.

Example communications through text include but are not limited to the following:

Customer Service/Support: Providing real-time assistance to customers regarding account and order inquiries. Messages include responses to user-initiated requests and follow-ups to ensure satisfaction. These may contain order status updates, troubleshooting steps, or answers to frequently asked questions.

Conversational: Two-way conversations with customers or people looking for service, including engagement, inquiries, and feedback.

Appointments: We receive appointment reminders and send/receive confirmations and send/receive cancellations to customers or people looking for service. These help ensure timely messages to help you stay informed about scheduled appointments and any changes, allowing you to adjust your plans accordingly.

Opting Out of Text Messages:

You have the right to opt out of receiving text messages from Wifi Squared, LLC at any time. To opt-out, you can reply “STOP” to any text message you receive from us.

E-newsletter

If you sign up for our email newsletter, your email address and any other details you provide are collected and stored by MailerLite, the third-party service we use to manage and send our mailing list. We use it to send you updates about Wifi Squared. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any newsletter we send, or by contacting us directly. The newsletter sign-up form is loaded on our pages by MailerLite, which may set its own cookies in your browser.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies of its own, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers — no Google Analytics, no advertising pixels, and no cross-site tracking of visitors.

Cookies may still be set by the third-party services described in this policy, most notably the MailerLite newsletter form. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Other services our pages load

To display our pages, your browser requests some files from other companies. Those companies can see your IP address and basic request information as a result. They are:

  • MailerLite — the newsletter sign-up form, on every page.
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces used across the site.
  • Unsplash — photographs shown on the home page.
  • Cloudflare — a graphics library used for the animated logo on the home page.

We do not send these companies your name, address, or any information you type into our forms.

Embedded content from other websites

Articles on this site may include embedded content (e.g. videos, images, articles, etc.). Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if the visitor has visited the other website.

These websites may collect data about you, use cookies, embed additional third-party tracking, and monitor your interaction with that embedded content, including tracking your interaction with the embedded content if you have an account and are logged in to that website.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell or share your personal information, mobile number, or usage data with third parties except where required by law or necessary to provide service — for example payment processors, network partners, or the service providers named in this policy who deliver our forms and email on our behalf.

How long we retain your data

We keep the information you send us for as long as needed to respond to your request and, if you become a customer, to provide and support your service and meet our record-keeping obligations. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

What rights you have over your data

You can review, update, or request deletion of your information by contacting us at connect@wifisquared.com or (865) 366-4006. This does not include data we are obliged to keep for administrative, legal, or security purposes.
