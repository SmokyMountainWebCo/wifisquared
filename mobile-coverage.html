Mobile Connect

Mobile Connect coverage

What determines coverage, how carriers are selected, and what we can and can't promise before you activate.

Mobile Connect coverage varies by carrier, location, terrain, building construction, network conditions, and compatible equipment. Wifi Squared evaluates the service location and normally selects one available carrier connection from Verizon, AT&T, or T-Mobile. The selected carrier may be changed if coverage or service conditions require it. A second simultaneously active or failover connection is a separate Mobile Connect service line. Coverage and specific performance are not guaranteed at every location. Contact Wifi Squared for an address- and device-specific review before activation.

How carrier selection works

Mobile Connect runs on established cellular networks rather than on a Wifi Squared tower. Before activation we look at your specific service address and choose one available carrier connection from Verizon, AT&T, or T-Mobile based on what actually performs there.

That choice is not permanent. If coverage or service conditions change, we may move your connection to a different carrier to keep the service working.

Service lines and pricing

Mobile Connect is priced per active service line at $97 per month. Most customers need one.

Bringing your own device

  • Compatibility must be approved before activation. Not every cellular router will work on the carrier we select for your address.
  • The first month's $97 payment is required before the SIM is sent.
  • You configure the device. If you'd like us to help, that assistance is billed under our service-call policy — $65 per hour plus applicable sales tax, one-hour minimum, then in 15-minute increments.

What Mobile Connect does not include

  • Cellular voice and SMS. Mobile Connect is a data service. It is not a phone plan and will not replace one.
  • A coverage guarantee. Cellular performance depends on things no provider controls — terrain, building materials, how busy the network is at that moment, and the equipment in use.
