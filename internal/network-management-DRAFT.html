This disclosure describes how Wifi Squared manages its network, what can affect the speeds you actually see, and how to reach us when something is not performing the way it should. It is provided so customers and prospective customers can make informed choices, and it is the document referenced by the Network Management line on our FCC Broadband Facts labels.

The speeds we advertise

Every plan is sold at an “up to” speed, not a guaranteed speed. Our fixed wireless plans are offered at up to 18, 50, 100, and 500 Mbps; Fiber Connect at up to 500 Mbps; and Mobile Connect at up to 100 Mbps. The speed available at a given address depends on distance from the tower site, line-of-sight, local tower capacity, the equipment installed, and conditions on the network at the time. This is why we check each address before recommending a plan rather than selling a tier sight-unseen.

We do not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free service, and the speed and uptime you experience will vary with network conditions, device quality, and location.

Data allowances

Our fixed wireless and fiber plans have no data caps. Mobile Connect includes unlimited data. We do not charge overage fees for additional data usage on any plan.

Congestion and excessive use

Wifi Squared serves customers from shared tower sites with finite capacity. Sustained excessive use by one account — for example, continuously consuming all available upload or download bandwidth — can degrade service for neighbors on the same tower. Where that happens, we may reduce the speed available to that account, and in persistent cases we may cancel the service.

Lawful use

Our Terms of Service prohibit using the service for illegal activity, including child exploitation material and illegal file sharing, and we cooperate with law enforcement when required. Restrictions on unlawful activity are not a form of traffic discrimination.

Outages

If an outage lasts more than 24 hours, we may issue a credit for the downtime. For shorter outages of an hour or more, we do our best to notify affected customers. If you believe an outage qualifies for a credit, contact us and we will review it.

Your information

We collect only the data needed to provide and maintain your service, such as name, address, contact details, network diagnostics, and billing information. We do not sell or share your personal information, mobile number, or usage data with third parties except where required by law or necessary to provide service. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.
