Common Questions

Answers before you even have to call

How does fixed wireless internet work?

We beam a signal from a local tower to a small antenna our tech mounts on your home or business. No phone lines, no cable runs, no digging up your yard — if there's line-of-sight to a tower, you can get connected.

Is fixed wireless better than satellite internet?

For most homes, yes. Our signal travels a few miles from a local tower — not 22,000 miles to orbit — so latency is low enough for video calls and online gaming. There are no data caps, and when you need support you talk to a local tech, not a national call center.

How much does your internet cost?

Essential is $49.99/mo for 25 Mbps, Plus is $69.99/mo for 50 Mbps, and Max is $89.99/mo for 100 Mbps. Every plan includes unlimited data. No contracts, no equipment rental games, no promo rates that double in year two.

Do you have data caps or contracts?

No and no. Every plan comes with unlimited data, and service is month-to-month. The price on the plan is the price on your bill.

Does weather affect the connection?

Our network is engineered for everyday East Tennessee weather. Because the signal travels to a nearby tower instead of through the entire atmosphere to a satellite, it holds up far better in rain and cloud cover. In a severe storm you might see a brief slowdown — and if anything acts up afterward, a local tech handles it fast.

Is it fast enough for working from home, streaming, and gaming?

Yes. Our Plus plan (50 Mbps) handles video calls and HD streaming on multiple TVs at once — one customer runs four TVs simultaneously with no issues. For 4K streaming and serious gaming, the Max plan (100 Mbps) with low latency is built for it.

How long does installation take?

First we run a free site survey to confirm line-of-sight and the speeds you'll actually get. The install itself typically takes 2–3 hours: a tech mounts a small antenna, runs clean weatherproofed cable, and sets up your router. You're online the same day.

What happens if I have a problem with my service?

Call us and a real local person answers — even after hours. Many issues can be fixed remotely, from speed tests to plan adjustments, and if it needs hands-on work we dispatch a tech quickly. We can also make sure you're on the right plan for how your household actually uses the internet.
